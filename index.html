How it works

STEP 01

Pick your spot

Eleven spots in three sizes — from a 20 × 14 mm badge to a 63 × 24 mm banner across the middle of the panel. Every spot shows its live high bid.

STEP 02

Place your bid

Send your bid through the form — company, logo, and amount. To take a spot that has a bid on it, beat it by at least 25%. You pay nothing until you win.

FAQ

Is this serious?
Completely. The iPhone duo costs $2,499 and I want the internet to pay for it, one sticker at a time. Marc Lou funded his HYROX race selling ad space on his body — this is the same idea, on the most-photographed gadget of 2026.
How many people will actually see my sticker?
The phone comes with me everywhere — cafés, coworking spaces, meetups, flights. Every unboxing photo, every video, and this site itself carry your logo with a link. Foldable iPhones get noticed; people ask to see them. Your sticker is part of that conversation.
What happens if I get outbid?
Nothing to undo — you only pay if you win. I'll email you that you've been outbid, and you can raise your bid on the same spot (or grab a different one) any time before pre-orders open on Oct 16.
Can I buy multiple spots?
Yes. Adjacent spots can even be merged into one bigger sticker — bid on each spot separately and mention the merge in your note.
What if the phone doesn't arrive on launch day?
Pre-orders ship in launch order — I'll be ordering at 5:00 AM PT sharp. If Apple slips the date, stickers go on whenever the phone lands, and the 12-month clock starts that day. You're never invoiced until the panel is real and in my hands.
