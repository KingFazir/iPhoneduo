How it works

STEP 01

Pick your spot

Eleven spots in three sizes — from a 17 × 11 mm badge to a 74 × 17 mm banner across the middle of the panel. Every spot shows its live high bid, and you can preview any sticker on it above.

STEP 02

Place your bid

Send your bid through the form — company, logo, and amount. To take a spot that has a bid on it, beat it by at least 25%. You pay nothing until you win.

STEP 03

Win the panel

Bidding closes when pre-orders open on 10.16. Winners get invoiced, stickers get printed, and when the duo ships on 10.23 your logo is on it from day one.

The rules

Bidding

  • Starting bids: Large $500 · Medium $250 · Small $100.
  • Outbid an existing bid by 25% or more to take the spot.
  • Highest bid per spot when pre-orders open on Oct 16 wins.
  • No payment up front — winners are invoiced on close.

Stickers

  • Your logo or mark, printed on premium cut vinyl at exact spot size.
  • Family-friendly brands only — I reserve the right to decline a bid.
  • Stays on the panel for at least 12 months or until the next iPhone.
  • The camera and the fold hinge are not for sale. Everything else is.

FAQ

Is this serious?
Completely. The iPhone duo starts at $1,999 and I want the internet to pay for it, one sticker at a time. Marc Lou funded his HYROX race selling ad space on his body — this is the same idea, on the most-photographed gadget of 2026.
How many people will actually see my sticker?
The phone comes with me everywhere — cafés, coworking spaces, meetups, flights. Every unboxing photo, every video, and this site itself carry your logo with a link. Foldable iPhones get noticed; people ask to see them. Your sticker is part of that conversation.
What happens if I get outbid?
Nothing to undo — you only pay if you win. I'll email you that you've been outbid, and you can raise your bid on the same spot (or grab a different one) any time before pre-orders open on Oct 16.
Can I buy multiple spots?
Yes. Adjacent spots can even be merged into one bigger sticker — bid on each spot separately and mention the merge in your note.
What if the phone doesn't arrive on time?
The duo ships October 23 and I'll be in the first pre-order wave. If Apple slips the date, stickers go on whenever the phone lands, and the 12-month clock starts that day. You're never invoiced until the panel is real and in my hands.
